Slide 2 — The Problem

Revenue leaks through more than one door

Merchants lose revenue across four channels, and not every recoverable rupee is worth equal effort to chase.

1. Failed Checkouts
Card declines, bank network timeouts, issuer switches, and insufficient customer account funds.
2. Partial Payments
Token advance paid, milestone deposit collected, but remaining installment balance abandoned.
3. Overdue Invoices
B2B commercial accounts receivable slipping well past contractual net-term due dates.
4. Refund Mismatches
Pre-authorized gateway funds captured at gateway level but left unreconciled against order records.
Slide 3 — The Solution

One engine that ranks opportunities, not just retries payments

Transforming raw delinquency events into an optimal, risk-bounded recovery action plan.

Phase 01
Detect
Continuously scans transaction events across all four opportunity types across checkouts, orders, invoices, and card authorizations.
Phase 02
Predict
Calibrated ML model estimates true recovery probability from historical behavior, retry counts, age, and payment rails.
Phase 03
Prioritize
Ranks opportunities by expected recovery value subject to a strict daily outreach effort limit via 0/1 Knapsack optimization.
Phase 04
Recover Safely
Deterministic guardrails approve, block, or route high-risk cases to manual review before dispatching live Razorpay links.

Safety Architecture
Guaranteed Bounded Execution

A high-value, low-confidence case never executes automatically. It is immediately quarantined for manual staff review. AI generates the explanation and root-cause diagnosis; a hard-coded deterministic rule decides whether it is safe to act.

Production Stack
Engineering Specifications

  • Razorpay Test SDK: Real payment links, smart switch, pre-auth capture
  • Idempotency & HMAC: Unique reference IDs and SHA256 webhook verification
