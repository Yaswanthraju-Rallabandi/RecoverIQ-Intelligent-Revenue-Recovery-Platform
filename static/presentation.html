DIAGNOSTIC MATRIX // SLIDE 02

Revenue leaks through more than one door

Merchants lose revenue across four distinct channels, and not every recoverable rupee is worth equal effort to chase.

01 / CHECKOUT FRICTION
Failed Checkouts
Card declines, issuer switch network timeouts, 3DS authentication drops, and insufficient bank balances during online checkout.
RISK: Naive retry storms trigger gateway penalties, spam merchant accounts, and frustrate buyers.
02 / MILESTONE DROP
Partial Payments
Customer pays initial token or booking advance, but subsequent milestone installments are neglected or abandoned.
RISK: Merchant manual chasing drops by 70% without automated, one-click payment links.
03 / RECEIVABLES SLIP
Overdue Invoices
Commercial B2B purchase invoices slipping past contractual 30/60/90 net terms with zero real-time dunning.
RISK: Working capital gets permanently locked in delinquent enterprise balances.
04 / SETTLEMENT HOLD
Refund Mismatches
Pre-authorized card authorizations captured on hold but unreconciled against order cancellations or partial refunds.
RISK: Unresolved holds tie up merchant liquidity and create dispute/chargeback exposure.
AI RECOVERY ARCHITECTURE // SLIDE 03

One engine that ranks opportunities, not just retries payments

A closed-loop decision system that converts unstructured payment exceptions into prioritized, safe, and audited actions.

STAGE 01 // DETECT
Multi-Vector Ingestion
Continuous scanner monitoring Razorpay checkout telemetry, invoices, and payment links. Normalizes heterogeneous payment records into unified recovery objects.
Live Webhooks + Cloud Polling
STAGE 02 // PREDICT
Calibrated Machine Learning
Gradient Boosted Decision Trees trained on empirical customer behavior, overdue age, payment method switch health, and transaction ticket size to estimate true recovery probability.
P(Recovery) in [0.0, 1.0]
STAGE 03 // PRIORITIZE
0/1 Knapsack DP
Solves dynamic programming allocation to maximize total expected yield E[V] under a strict daily merchant effort budget constraint W. Eliminates wasteful greedy sorting.
Max SUM(x_i * EV_i) s.t. w <= W
STAGE 04 // RECOVER SAFELY
Deterministic Guardrails
Hard-coded safety firewall verifies spend caps, switch latency, and spam backoff before dispatching actions. Quarantines low-confidence, high-risk cases for human staff review.
Zero Hallucination Firewall

ISOLATION ARCHITECTURE

Bounded AI Safety Layer

"A high-value, low-confidence case never executes automatically."

  • Decoupled AI:ML models output probability vectors; hard-coded rules decide actionability.
  • Deterministic Rules:Enforces maximum retry spend, cooldown windows, and switch latency caps.
  • Auto-Quarantine:Transactions >Rs 10,000 with confidence < 70% route to human review queue.
  • Zero Hallucination:Mathematical bounds guarantee no fabricated recovery actions or unauthorized retries.

OPTIMIZATION BENCHMARKS // SLIDE 05

Not a sorted list — a solved allocation problem

Greedy FIFO sorting exhausts operational capacity on low-probability cases. RecoverIQ solves 0/1 Knapsack Dynamic Programming.

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION
Maximize SUM(x_i * EV_i) subject to SUM(x_i * w_i) <= W_daily, where x_i in {0, 1}

Under a fixed merchant outreach capacity of W = 6 effort units per day, the engine computes the exact combination of actions maximizing total expected recovery yield E[V] = P(Recovery) * Amount - Outreach_Cost.

  • [+] PARTIAL-INV-002:Rs 9,520 | Effort: 2 Units | 78% Prob -> EV: Rs 7,425

[-] INV-2026-003 (Rs 15,200) — Exceeds remaining budget (requires 2 units, 0 remaining). Lower EV-per-effort density than chosen combination. Plain sorting would have naively picked this and sacrificed Rs 13,000+ in other recoveries.
